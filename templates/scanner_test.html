Scanner test

Open this page on the phone you will scan with, using the shop's normal https address. It checks the camera and the scanner, then lets you scan a real barcode. It takes about a minute. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The camera cannot be tested from a computer or from the shop's server. Only a real phone can show that a barcode is read, which is why this page exists.
