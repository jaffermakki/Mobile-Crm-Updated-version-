{{ icon('shopping-cart') }} Selling at the counter

  1. Open Point of Sale. Scan a barcode with a USB scanner, or tap Camera and point the phone at the barcode. You can also type a SKU or tap a product.
  2. Selling a phone? A box appears on the line for its IMEI. Tap the small scan button next to it and scan the barcode on the box, or type it. Dial *#06# on the phone to see its IMEI.
  3. Pick the customer (optional), choose how they pay, and finish the sale.

If the IMEI looks wrong (the wrong length, or it fails the check-digit test) you get a yellow warning. It never stops the sale, because some phones have serial numbers instead.

{{ icon('undo-2') }} Taking back some of the items on a sale

  1. Open the invoice (from Invoices) and tap Return items.
  2. Set how many of each item the customer is bringing back. Untick Back on the shelf for anything damaged or defective, so it is not counted as stock you can sell again.
  3. Choose how the money goes back (cash from the drawer, back to the card on the terminal, store credit, or e-transfer) and tap Review the refund.
  4. Check the amount, then tap Confirm return & refund. Nothing is saved until you do.

The customer gets back what they actually paid for those items, including their share of any discount and of each tax. You can return a sale in several pieces; together they never come to more than the sale. Cash Up and the card check count the return on the day it happens. Once part of a sale has been returned, the whole-invoice Process Refund and exchanges are switched off for it; use Return items for whatever is left.

{{ icon('wrench') }} Taking in a repair

  1. Open Repairs and fill in the customer details.
  2. Device: pick the brand, then pick the model from the list (iPhones and iPads, Samsung phones, Galaxy M phones and Galaxy Tabs, Google Pixels including the Pixel 10 and 11 and the Pixel Tablet, and Motorola are included). If the model is not in the list, choose Not in the list — type it, or type straight into the box.
  3. IMEI: tap the scan button and scan the barcode on the phone's box or back, or type the number.
  4. Save the ticket. On the ticket, take Photos of the front, the back and any damage before the repair starts.
  5. Use Record check-in to note the phone's condition when it arrives (what works, what is damaged, what was left with you, the passcode, and whether Find My or a Google lock is on). Then Print check-in sheet for the customer to sign.

{{ icon('shield-check') }} Asking the customer to approve a repair price

  1. Open the repair ticket and find Quote approval. Enter the price (before tax), what the work is, an optional message, and how long the quote is good for.
  2. Tap Text the quote to the customer's name. They get a text with a private link. If the customer has no phone number, or asked not to be texted, tap Just make the link and send it yourself with Copy.
  3. The customer opens the link and taps Approve this quote or Decline. They see only your shop name, the ticket number, the device, the work and the price. You get a bell notification, and the ticket shows the answer.
  4. If they tell you in person or by phone instead, tap They approved or They declined on the ticket to record it.

Approving sets the ticket's price to the quote, but it does not move the repair to the next stage; you still decide when work starts. Only the first answer counts, and a new quote replaces the old one, so the old link stops working. Use Withdraw to cancel a quote that has not been answered. Opening the link does not answer it.

{{ icon('smartphone') }} Trade-ins

Open Trade-Ins — the device and IMEI boxes work the same way as on a repair ticket: pick the brand and model from the list, and scan or type the IMEI.

{{ icon('scan-barcode') }} Using the phone camera to scan

  • The first time, the browser asks to use the camera. Tap Allow. If you tapped Block by mistake, open the browser's site settings and allow the camera for this site.
  • The camera only works on a secure (https) address. The live site is secure; a plain http address on the shop network will not open the camera.
  • Hold the barcode flat, fill about half the frame, and keep the light off the glare. It beeps and the code is filled in.
  • A small IMEI label on a phone's box or back is the hardest one: hold the phone about 15 to 25 cm away (any closer and the camera cannot focus), use good light, and use Zoom or Light if they show. If it still will not read, tap Take a photo instead, take a close, sharp picture of the label with the phone's own camera, and it is read from the photo. You can always type the number.
  • If a barcode is new to the system, you are offered a chance to link it to a product so the next scan works by itself.

{{ icon('scan-barcode') }} Test the scanner on this phone Open Help on the phone you scan with, tap this, and follow the four steps. It shows exactly what is wrong if scanning does not work.

{{ icon('truck') }} Receiving a purchase order

  1. Open Purchasing and open the order that was sent to the supplier.
  2. When the delivery arrives, tap Scan items as they arrive. Every line starts at 0; each scan adds 1, up to the ordered quantity. Each line shows how many of the order have been counted.
  3. An item that is not on this order is rejected so it cannot be received by mistake. A barcode the system does not know can be linked to a product on the spot.
  4. Tap Confirm Receipt — Add to Stock. If any line is short, you are asked to confirm. Nothing is added to stock until you do. Back to the full order returns to the normal list.

{{ icon('banknote') }} End of the day

  1. Took cash out of the drawer today (supplies, a lunch run, paying a supplier in cash, a bank deposit)? Record it on Expenses with Record cash out / expense, choosing Cash from the till as where the money came from. Do this before you count the drawer, so the expected total is right.
  2. Open Cash Up, count the drawer and enter the Actual Cash Counted and the card terminal's batch total.
  3. Open Moneris Check. Enter the terminal's batch total, then tick each card payment off against the terminal's list. Anything that does not match is shown under Needs a look.

{{ icon('shield-check') }} Staying safe

  • Use your own PIN and never share it. Change it any time under Security. Choose six random digits, not 1111 or 1234.
  • The owner should turn on 2-step sign-in on the Security page.
  • Always Log out on a shared phone or computer.

  • Use Download Full Backup in Settings regularly and keep a copy away from the shop, or switch on the Sunday backup email.
